# NovaCart Case Study – Sales & Profit Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This case study focuses on analyzing NovaCart's sales data to understand overall business performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and regional trends.

The analysis was performed using **Power BI** to transform raw data into an interactive dashboard and generate actionable business insights.

## 🎯 Business Problem

NovaCart wanted to understand:

* How sales and profit are performing overall
* Which products and categories contribute most to revenue and profit
* Which regions and customers are performing well or poorly
* Where potential business problems or opportunities exist
* What actions could improve overall business performance

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains transactional sales information including fields related to:

* Orders and customers
* Products and categories
* Sales and profit
* Quantity
* Regions and locations
* Order and shipping information

## 🛠️ Tools Used

* **Power BI** – Data analysis, DAX measures & dashboard creation
* **Power Query** – Data preparation and validation
* **Excel** – Initial data inspection and validation

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

* Overall **Sales, Profit, Orders and Quantity** analysis
* **Sales & Profit by Category and Product**
* **Regional performance** analysis
* **Customer performance** analysis
* Identification of **high-performing and underperforming areas**
* Analysis of trends and business KPIs through interactive Power BI visuals

## 💡 Key Insights

The dashboard helped identify differences in performance across **regions, categories, products, and customers**, highlighting areas that contribute strongly to revenue as well as areas where profitability or sales performance can be improved.

The analysis also helped identify **regional return-rate concerns**, providing an opportunity for NovaCart to investigate the reasons behind higher returns and take corrective action.

## 📈 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, NovaCart should:

* Focus on high-performing products and categories while reviewing low-profit products.
* Investigate regions with weaker profitability or higher return rates.
* Identify the reasons behind customer returns and improve product/service quality accordingly.
* Use customer and regional performance data to support targeted business decisions.
* Continuously monitor key KPIs through dashboards for better decision-making.
